    How it works

    1. Spot it. You see someone getting bullied in the comments.
    2. Call in backup. Share the link. A moderator checks it before it goes live.
    3. Show up. Leave a kind comment on the post, then tap "I commented on the post."

    Meet the crew

    Rules of engagement

    • Only kind comments. Never reply to or attack the bullies.
    • Talk about the person's post, not the drama.
    • Don't mention Backup Crew or say someone sent you. Just leave a kind comment like anyone would.
    • Report bullying comments to the platform too.
    • If someone may be in danger, tell a trusted adult or call 911.
    • If someone talks about hurting themselves, call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

    Privacy & safety

    What Backup Crew saves

    • The link to the post you call in backup for, and which app it's on.
    • Your short description of what's happening (up to 240 characters).
    • Whether the post is your own or someone else's.
    • Which posts you've marked "I commented on the post" or reported.

    What it never asks for

    • No names, emails, phone numbers, passwords or locations.
    • The form asks you not to include names or usernames of anyone involved.

    Who can see what

    • Everyone sees approved posts and how many crew members showed up.
    • Only moderators see posts waiting for approval and how many times a post was reported.
    • Other users never see what you reported.

    How posts are checked

    • Only links from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Facebook, Reddit, Snapchat and Twitch are accepted.
    • A moderator reviews every post before it goes live, and removes posts that are fake, harmful or target someone.
    • Each person can add up to 5 posts per hour, and the same post can't be added twice.
    • Posts move to Completed after 30 days, or once 50 crew members have shown up.

    Staying safe

    • Never share your own personal information in a comment.
    • Don't reply to or argue with the bullies. Report their comments to the app instead.
    • If someone may be in danger, tell a trusted adult or call 911.
    • If someone talks about hurting themselves, call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
