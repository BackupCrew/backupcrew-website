Backup Crew Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Backup Crew is a free app and website where students call in backup for someone being bullied online, and the crew leaves kind comments on the real post. Keeping young people safe is the reason Backup Crew exists. These standards apply to the Backup Crew Android app and the website at backupcrew.org.

Backup Crew has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Any content, link, description, or note that sexualizes, grooms, exploits, or endangers a child is prohibited and will be removed.

What is prohibited

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including links to it.
  • Sexualizing minors in posts, descriptions, or thank-you notes.
  • Grooming, or using Backup Crew to contact, befriend, or pressure a child for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, sharing private images, or threatening to share them.
  • Sharing a child's private information, such as where they live or go to school.

How Backup Crew prevents it

  • Backup Crew has no private messaging between users.
  • Users don't create profiles and are never asked for names, photos, or locations.
  • Only links to posts on major social media apps are accepted, and no images can be uploaded.
  • A moderator reviews every post and every thank-you note before anyone else can see it.
  • Posts that are fake, harmful, or target someone are rejected or removed.

How to report a concern

  1. In the app: tap Report on any post. Moderators review every report.
  2. By email: write to backupcrew4u@gmail.com with the subject "Backup Crew child safety report" and the link to the post.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 first. You can also report child sexual exploitation directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.

What we do when we learn of CSAE

  • Remove the content from Backup Crew as soon as we learn about it.
  • Report apparent CSAM to the NCMEC CyberTipline, as required by U.S. law.
  • Cooperate with law enforcement requests.
  • Do not share or forward the material to anyone except as required to report it.

Compliance with the law

Backup Crew follows applicable child safety laws and regulations, including reporting confirmed CSAM to NCMEC.

Child safety point of contact

For child safety questions or notices, including notices from Google Play, contact: backupcrew4u@gmail.com
