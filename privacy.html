Backup Crew Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Backup Crew ("we," "us") is a free app and website where students call in backup for someone being bullied online, and the crew leaves kind comments on the real post. This policy explains what information Backup Crew collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the Backup Crew Android app and the website at backupcrew.org.

Short version: Backup Crew does not ask for your name, email, phone number, password, or location. It has no ads and does not sell or share your information for advertising.

Information we collect

Information you choose to submit:

  • The link to a social media post you call in backup for, and which app it is on.
  • Your short description of what is happening (up to 240 characters), and whether the post is your own or someone else's.
  • Which posts you mark "I commented on the post," posts you report, and any thank-you notes you send.

The form asks you not to include names or usernames of anyone involved. Please do not put personal information in descriptions or notes.

Information collected automatically:

  • A random user ID. When you open Backup Crew, you are signed in anonymously through Google Firebase. This ID is not linked to your name or email. It lets the app remember which posts you added, joined, or reported, and enforce limits such as 5 posts per hour.
  • Basic technical information that Google Firebase processes to run the service, such as your device's IP address, which is used to connect to and secure the service.
  • Your Night ops (dark mode) setting, saved only on your own device.

Moderators: Moderators sign in with a Google account on the website so they can approve posts. For moderators only, we receive the email address of that Google account.

How we use information

  • To show approved posts and how many crew members have shown up.
  • To let moderators review posts before they go live and remove posts that are fake, harmful, or target someone.
  • To prevent spam and abuse, for example by limiting how many posts one person can add and blocking duplicate posts.

Who can see what

  • Everyone using Backup Crew can see approved posts (the link, app name, and description) and how many crew members showed up.
  • Only moderators can see posts waiting for approval and how many times a post was reported.
  • Other users never see what you reported.

Sharing

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. Backup Crew uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to sign you in anonymously and store app data. Google processes this data on our behalf to provide the service. You can read about Firebase's privacy practices at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep information

Posts move to Completed after 30 days, or once 50 crew members have shown up. Information is kept while Backup Crew is running unless you ask us to delete it (see below) or a moderator removes it.

How to delete your Backup Crew data

Backup Crew does not have accounts, so you don't need to log in to delete your data. To request deletion:

  1. Email backupcrew4u@gmail.com with the subject "Backup Crew data deletion."
  2. Include the link to each post you added, or describe the posts, reports, or thank-you notes you want deleted.
  3. We will delete them and reply within 30 days.

What gets deleted: the posts you added (link, app name, and description), your "I commented on the post" marks, your reports, and your thank-you notes.

What is kept: nothing you asked us to delete. We do not keep extra copies of deleted posts, marks, reports, or notes.

You can also uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's settings, to remove your anonymous ID from your device.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with Google Firebase, which protects data with industry-standard security. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't submit personal information.

Children

Backup Crew is meant for people 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted information, email us and we will delete it.

Staying safe

Never share your own personal information in a comment. Don't reply to or argue with bullies. Report their comments to the app instead. If someone may be in danger, tell a trusted adult or call 911. If someone talks about hurting themselves, call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Read our Child Safety Standards.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
